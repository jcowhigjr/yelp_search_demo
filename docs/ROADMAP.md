# Roadmap & Backlog

Maintained collaboratively by the engineering team and whichever agent is
driving — via the `/groom-backlog` skill (`.agents/skills/groom-backlog/SKILL.md`).

This is the durable record of *decisions*, not a mirror of GitHub Issues. If a
fact lives fine in the issue tracker, leave it there. What belongs here is the
reasoning that would otherwise evaporate: why this order, what we decided not to
build, and which constraint is blocking an estimate.
